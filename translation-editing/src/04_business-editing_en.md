## Summary

| Category | Example (before → after) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Spelling | independant → independent; recieve → receive; collegue → colleague; alot → (removed) | Typos and non-words undermine a first impression. |
| Grammar | We was founded → We were founded; a startup who helps → that helps; which met → who met; "every employee … your" → "their" | Subject–verb agreement; "who" for people, "that" for companies. |
| Apostrophes | dont → don't; marketplace's → marketplaces; Courier's → Couriers; there → their | Possessive vs. plural vs. contraction. |
| Style | leveraging real-time data and synergies → uses real-time data | Jargon replaced with a concrete claim. |
| Structure | two titles → one; bullets made parallel; headings added ("Routines", "Our values") | Easier to scan on the first day. |
| Consistency | 4pm / 9:30 / "between 11 and 15 (CET)" → 4 p.m. / 9:30 a.m. / 11:00 and 15:00 CET; "6" → "six"; "2-factor" → "two-factor" | One time format; numbers under ten spelled out in running text. |
| Tone | !!, !!!, "do not hesitate to contact with us at any time of day or night" → neutral, direct sentences | Warm but professional; no unintended promises. |
| Wording | "expect from everyone to be available" → "expect everyone to be available"; "send until" → "sent by" | Natural collocations. |
