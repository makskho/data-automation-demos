# Copywriting samples

Three short Russian-language texts for one imaginary client, written to a synthetic brief. They exist
so that a text order (article, social post, ad offer) has something concrete to point at.

The client is invented: a small garden-design studio that gets most orders in spring. No real
company, person, or client file is behind it, and no biography or past experience is implied.

## The three pieces

| File | Format | Length of the text | Brief it was written to |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`01-статья.md`](01-статья.md) | blog article | 3 000–4 000 characters | practical, neutral: how to tidy a client spreadsheet before the season; headline, lead, subheadings, checklist, closing paragraph |
| [`02-пост.md`](02-пост.md) | social-media post | 600–900 characters | conversational, one call to action (read the article), at most two emoji |
| [`03-оффер.md`](03-оффер.md) | ad offer / landing hero block | up to 600 characters | headline, subheadline, three benefit bullets, one button; no guarantees, discounts or numbers |

Each file starts with its brief (3–4 lines), then a `---` line, then the text itself. Lengths are
counted on the text after that line, spaces included.

## What the samples show

- **Structure.** The article opens with the situation, gives one action per subheading, and ends on
  a checklist and one habit worth keeping. The offer puts the promise first and the action last.
- **Clarity.** Plain words, concrete steps, no filler openings and no closing recaps.
- **Tone for the platform.** The same subject is handled three ways: an explanatory blog voice, a
  light community-page voice with a joke against the writer's own studio, and a compact sales block.

## Limits

- Samples were written to a synthetic brief. They are not client work.
- No claim of past clients, sales results, reach, conversions, or A/B outcomes is made or implied.
- Nothing here is evidence of subject-matter expertise. The topic is deliberately neutral.
- Health, finance and legal topics are avoided on purpose: such texts need the client's own facts
  and sources, and a sample cannot supply them.
- The texts contain no statistics, quotes, prices or guarantees, because none can be backed up here.
- Russian only. Other languages are a separate sample, not implied by this one.
